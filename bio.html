Research & Innovation

One of my proudest achievements is publishing my first-author paper at the IEEE International Conference on Communications (ICC) 2024. The paper, titled "SK-SVR-CNN: A Hybrid Approach for Traffic Flow Prediction with Signature PDE Kernel and Convolutional Neural Networks," represents months of rigorous research and innovation.

This work, conducted under the supervision of Prof. Peng Sun and Prof. Azzedine Boukerche, demonstrates my ability to combine theoretical knowledge with practical applications. The research focused on developing novel approaches to traffic flow prediction, leveraging the mathematical properties of signature kernels to capture temporal dependencies in traffic data.

Research Impact: This work has contributed to the field of intelligent transportation systems and showcases my commitment to advancing knowledge through rigorous scientific inquiry.
